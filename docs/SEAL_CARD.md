**Flight model:** B3 limits & stall (B2 lift & pitch) — full **3-DOF point mass**. Flight-path angle **γ is a stored state** (per-aircraft state is now the 7-tuple `lat,lon,psi,phi,alt,tas,gamma`), driven by commanded load factor `n` (`Command.target_g`). Altitude is **earned** (`alṫ = V·sin γ`); pulling g raises induced drag → bleeds speed. Generalizes B1 (`n=1/cosφ, γ=0` ⇒ the old `ψ̇=g₀tanφ/V` level turn). Builds on B1 energy (TAS from thrust−drag). V_MIN=30 m/s. **B3 (v1.7r0):** the achievable `n` is bounded per-airframe by BOTH the structural g limits (`n_min_struct`/`n_max_struct`) AND the C_Lmax aerodynamic ceiling `n_aero = cl_max*qS/(m*g0)` — `n = clamp(n_cmd, max(n_min_struct,-n_aero), min(n_max_struct,n_aero))`. Below the corner speed `V* = stall*sqrt(n_max_struct)` the aero ceiling binds and the turn collapses (**accelerated stall**); the 1 g stall speed falls out for free. Retires the B2 global placeholder [-3,9]; post-stall lift held at C_Lmax (no departure). No new det_math (det_sin/det_cos only). **B4 (v1.8r0):** **per-airframe aero retune (data-only mechanism, but moves goldens → reseal).** The envelopes carried the un-calibrated v1.2r0 aero, so emergent level top speeds ran ~30 % low (109–142 m/s).
