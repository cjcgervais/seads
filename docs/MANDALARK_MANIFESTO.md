## 2. Mode d'emploi — the operating law, distilled

Each clause below was paid for in this repository's own history; none is aspiration.
The ledgers cite where.

1. **Primary sources over echoes.** An agent's summary of data is a claim; the
   artifact is the fact. Every ruling that can be checked against an artifact must be,
   before it is trusted — and archiving the artifact is a condition of ruling on it.
   (Paid for four times over: Golden #3's mis-identified capture; the plane's
   gravity→crab overturn; the eagle's crab→camera→pointing-law chain; and the
   34-row throw tape correcting its own adjudication echo within the hour.)
2. **Instrument before ranking.** No attribution is ranked, no lever is tuned, until
   its mechanism has been measured by an instrument that models what the pilot
   actually flies — both hands, real maneuvers, duration-stated predicates. A
   confident story, including the founder's own hypothesis, gets the instrument
   treatment; that is what makes the confirmed ones unarguable.
3. **Pre-register the decision rule before the numbers exist**, including the outcome
   nobody expects (the P0 rider), so no result can be rationalised after the fact.
4. **The founder's words are the specification.** Captured verbatim, never paraphrased
   into mechanism language and then reasoned from. Feel is the top of the cascade;
   code is the bottom; the four-level cascade docs are the translation layer, in both
   directions.
5. **Two frontier agents, two domains, one verification layer.** The engineer owns the
   code tree; the kernel official (docs agent) owns the ledgers, goldens, and rulings.
   Each checks the other's work from their own seat; they hand off through the founder
   and through committed artifacts, never through trust alone. Domain separation is
   what makes the check real.
6. **Honest supersession, plainly stated.** When an attribution or a ruling is
   overturned by measurement, the record says so in plain words where the original
   lives — nobody is embarrassed; each landed fix is also the experiment that sharpens
   the next attribution. Sacred values (tuning captures, goldens) are append-only:
   supersede, never overwrite.
7. **Character is a ruling, never a default.** Every vessel (plane, eagle, whatever
   comes) gets its own character sheet, its own ledger, its own constants — chosen on
   the founder's stick, recorded with his verdict, revisable only by his word.
8. **The repo is the proxy engine.** Preservation is not overhead: the meaning and
   flavor of the productions must be observable through the record, and the record
   must double as data for pragmatic ends. Wash, rinse, repeat — a clean
   high-performance engine of intent and fun-maxing.

## 3. Standing

This manifesto governs the spirit in which every ledger entry, seal, golden, consult,
and ruling in this repository is made. Where a procedural document (CLAUDE.md, the
sealing checklist, a vessel ledger) states a narrower rule, the narrower rule wins on
mechanics; this file states why the mechanics exist. It is chronicled as the
inception-era record of the Mandalark Gaming Engine, written while the methodology it
describes was solving the eagle's curl in real time.
