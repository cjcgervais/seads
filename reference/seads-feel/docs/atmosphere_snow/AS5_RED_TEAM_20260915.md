## P1-A -- the AS-4 red-team's lattice-decoupling fix is a measured NO-OP, and its test auto-disarmed

`kFlurryFillGauge = 0.85` (`render/snowfall.cpp:36`) was measured and validated against a
**16-cell / 2.5 deg** squall lattice ("worst 0.222 over 14/16/18/20 squall cells"). AS-5
moved the squall to **3 cells at 8 deg inner** -- outside that validated range entirely. At
3 vs 5 cells the first three golden-angle *bearings* coincide exactly, and the radial
offsets the gauge produces (2.6-5.7 deg) are now smaller than the squall's 8 deg inner disc.

The test prints `un-gauged 0.600 -> gauged 0.600 (chance 0.600)`: **the gauge moves nothing.**

Worse, this same commit changed the bar from an absolute `gauged < 0.35` to
`gauged <= chance + 0.1` (`test/unit/test_snowfall.cpp:551`) -- **a baseline that rises with
the defect, so it cannot fail.**

**CONFIRMED BY MUTATION:** setting `kFlurryFillGauge = 1.0` -- deleting the AS-4 fix outright
-- and rebuilding, the entire snowfall suite passes **70094/70094 with a byte-identical
printout**.

Consequence: 3 of 5 flurry cells per dome are buried inside a squall cell, so the light
band's `max()` hides. That is plausibly the real cause of the taper drop 0.68 -> 0.56, which
was absorbed **by moving a bar rather than by attribution**.

Trap classes: "config-relative bounds or silent disarm" + "a golden only pins the code that
recorded it" + "a gate factor that is EXACTLY 1 on the tested path", in one.

**Fix (AS-6):** re-derive the gauge against the shipped squall geometry (or separate by
bearing offset now that the counts are coprime-small), and RESTORE AN ABSOLUTE BAR.

`anchor_cell_center`): budget(0) = 0.2357 -- two cells fully on, a third 65% on. Over in-dome
directions at t=0:

| | pre-AS-5 | AS-5 shipped |
|---|---|---|
| has snow at spawn | 0.000 | **0.63** |
| has HEAVY snow (>0.5) at spawn | 0.000 | **0.36** |

The lane recorded R2 as "spawn *can* start inside a live squall". **It is a coin flip, not
an edge case.** And it collapses R1 and R2 into ONE frame: the haze budget at t=0 is
*exactly* 0, so the shipped spawn look is **heavy snow falling out of a provably clear sky.**
They were recorded as two independent readings; they are the same bug.

**Fix (AS-6):** `squall_phase_off ~ 1.3-1.5` -- the lever the commit deliberately left
undialled -- puts t=0 on the squall budget's minimum (budget 0.0031, below the lowest cell
